Sunday weigh-in rule

Same morning, after the toilet, before food. If you have lost less than 0.25 kg a week for 3 weeks running, trim about 100–150 kcal a day. If you are losing more than 1 kg a week, add food back. Also note your waist and your lift weights, because muscle gain can hide fat loss on the scale.

Dinner plate

  • Half the plate veg or salad
  • Palm and a half of protein
  • One fist of carbs (rice, pasta, potato)
  • Take your share once, no seconds. Extra veg if still hungry.
Cheap shop for breakfasts and lunches
  • Eggs (tray of 15–30), porridge oats, milk
  • Own-brand Greek-style yoghurt, bananas, apples
  • Wholemeal bread and wraps, rice, pasta
  • Tinned tuna, beans and lentils
  • Chicken thighs, frozen veg and peas
  • Onions and carrots; cheap whey protein if budget allows
  • Check yellow stickers and own-brand ranges first
